For my thesis I designed a multi-site enterprise network and built it in simulation, in Cisco Packet Tracer.

The headquarters has a redundant core with HSRP, and OSPF ties the sites together. Public-facing servers sit in a DMZ behind an ASA firewall, a wireless LAN controller runs the Wi-Fi and DHCP hands out the addresses. Every part was tested.
